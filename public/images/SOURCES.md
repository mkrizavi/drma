# Photography sources

The service and hero photos are displayed from Unsplash's image CDN and are marked free to use under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). They illustrate the type of property or equipment; they do not depict DRMA projects, staff, or Munich locations. Source links and photographer credits are maintained in `src/lib/photos.ts`.

The owner portrait `davor-drmic.webp` was supplied by the owner. It was rotated according to its camera orientation and resized for web delivery; his appearance was not altered.
